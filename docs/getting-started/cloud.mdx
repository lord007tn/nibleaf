This tutorial takes you from a new account to a small, searchable public site.
It takes about 10 minutes and does not need a credit card.

## Before you start

You need an email address you can open, or a Google account, and an up-to-date
browser. Nibleaf Cloud is free during its beta. Don't use this tutorial site for
confidential material.

## Create your account

1. Open [Nibleaf sign up](https://nibleaf.com/sign-up).
2. Enter your name and email, accept the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, and
   select **Create account**. You can also select **Create account with Google**.
3. If you used email, enter the one-time code we sent you. The code works once
   and expires after 10 minutes. Select **Resend code** if it doesn't arrive, and
   check your spam folder.

Nibleaf creates a starter site called **Documentation** for you and publishes
it, so you can see a working example straight away. It has three sample pages:
**Getting started**, **Installation**, and **Components tour**.

## Name your site

1. Open your site from **Sites**. You land on the **Site overview**.
2. Open **Settings → General**.
3. Change **Name** and **Description**, then save. The name appears in the site
   navigation and the browser tab.

The **Site address** field on the same screen sets your free Nibleaf web
address. To use your own domain later, see
[Connect a custom domain](/product/custom-domains).

## Write two useful pages

1. Select **Open editor**.
2. Open the **Getting started** page and replace the sample text. Say what the
   docs cover, who they are for, and the first thing a reader can achieve.
3. Select **New page** to add a second page, for example **First task**. Describe
   one real task, what the reader should see when it worked, and what to do if
   it didn't.
4. On the first page, link to the second page.

The editor saves as you type and shows **Draft saved**. Wait for it before you
leave the page. To learn the editor, read [Write and organize pages](/product/authoring).
You can delete the sample pages you don't need from the **More options** menu.

## Preview and publish

1. Select **Preview** to open your draft in a new tab. If nothing opens, allow
   pop-ups for Nibleaf.
2. Check the link between your pages and how the pages look on a narrow window.
3. Select **Publish**. Review the list of changes and select **Publish now**.
4. Wait until the dialog says **Your changes are live**, then select **View site**.

## Check what readers see

Open your site's address in a private browser window, where you are signed out.
You can find the address on the **Site overview** under **Your site's address**.
Then:

- open both pages;
- search for a phrase from the second page;
- open a made-up address on your site and confirm you see a "page not found"
  message.

When all three work, your site is ready to share.
