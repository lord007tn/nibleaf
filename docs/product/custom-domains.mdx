Every site starts with a free Nibleaf address. You can also serve it from your
own address, such as `docs.example.com`. Nibleaf sets up the secure connection
(HTTPS) for you and renews it automatically.

## Before you start

- You need to be the site's Owner or an Admin.
- You need access to the DNS settings where your domain is registered, such as
  your domain registrar or hosting company. DNS (Domain Name System) is the
  address book that tells browsers where your domain lives. If someone else
  manages your domain, ask them to add the records for you.
- Publish your site and check that it works at its free address first.
- Use a subdomain, such as `docs.example.com`, rather than your main website
  address.

## Add your domain

1. Open **Settings → Custom domain**.
2. Enter your domain, for example `docs.example.com`, and select **Add domain**.
3. Nibleaf shows **Add these DNS records at your provider**, a list of records
   with a **Type**, **Name**, and **Value**:
   - the **CNAME** record points your address to your Nibleaf site;
   - the **TXT** record proves you own the domain.
4. In another tab, open your domain provider's DNS settings and add each record.
   Use **Copy DNS record** to avoid typing mistakes. Most providers want the short
   **Name** shown. Use the full name only if your provider asks for it.
5. Save the records at your provider, then come back and select **Check again**.

## Wait for the connection

DNS changes usually take a few minutes, but they can take up to 24 hours. While
you wait, the domain shows **Waiting for DNS — this can take up to 24 hours**.

When the records are found and the secure connection is ready, the domain shows
**Connected — secure (HTTPS) ✓**. Open the address in a private browser window to
confirm your site appears.

## Make it your main address

If you connect more than one domain, select **Make primary** on the one readers
should use. You can do this only after the domain shows it is connected and
secure. The primary domain appears on your site overview under
**Your site's address**. Invitations to [private readers](/private-reader-access)
also use it.

## If something goes wrong

- **Needs attention:** open **Configuration** to see which record is missing or
  wrong. Compare each **Type**, **Name**, and **Value** with your provider's
  settings, fix them, and select **Check again**.
- **The domain is already connected:** the address is used by another site.
  Remove it there first.
- **Still waiting after 24 hours:** check for an old record with the same name at
  your provider and remove it, then select **Check again**.

To stop using a domain, select **Remove**. Your site stops working at that
address straight away, but it keeps working at its free Nibleaf address.

On a self-hosted Nibleaf instance, custom domains need setup by your operator
first; see [Take a self-hosted installation to production](/self-hosting/production).
