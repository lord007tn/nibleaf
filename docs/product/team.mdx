Each site has its own team. Inviting someone to one site does not give them
access to your other sites. You manage the team in **Settings → Members**.

## Choose a role

| Role | What they can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Everything an Admin can do, plus transfer ownership. Each site has exactly one Owner. |
| **Admin** | Manage site settings and the team: invite and remove people, change roles, connect domains, and set up AI, add-ons, and integrations. Also writes and publishes. |
| **Editor** | Write, comment, preview, and publish pages. Editors can also see the team, manage languages, check usage, create one-time exports, and create their own AI assistant keys. |

Give people the smallest role that lets them do their job. Most writers only need
**Editor**.

If an Editor opens a settings section reserved for Admins, Nibleaf shows
**Administrator access required** and suggests asking an Owner or Admin.

## Invite a teammate

You need to be an Owner or Admin.

1. Open **Settings → Members**.
2. Under **Invite by email**, enter the person's email address and choose a role:
   **Admin** or **Editor**.
3. Select **Invite**.

Nibleaf creates the invitation and emails it to the person, unless member
invitation emails are turned off in **Settings → Notifications**. You can also
select **Copy invite link** and send the link yourself.

The person opens the link and signs in, or creates an account with the invited
email address. An invitation expires after 7 days.

To check who hasn't joined yet, look under **Pending invitations**. Select
**Revoke invitation** to stop an invitation link from working.

## Change a role or remove someone

- To change a role, choose a new one in the person's **Role** menu.
- To remove someone, select **Remove member**. They lose access to the site's
  docs and settings straight away.

You can't give someone a role higher than your own, and you can't change or
remove someone who ranks above you.

## Transfer ownership

Only the Owner can do this, and the new Owner must already be an Admin.

1. If needed, change the person's role to **Admin** first.
2. Select **Transfer ownership** next to their name and confirm.

You become an Admin, and they become the Owner.

## When someone leaves

Remove the person from every site they worked on. Their personal AI assistant
keys and connected apps stop working on their next request; see
[Work on your docs with an AI assistant](/product/ai-assistants).
