Your **organization** owns your websites, and its people are its **members**.
Each member has an organization role and, unless they are an Owner or Admin,
access to the websites you choose, with a role on each.

You manage people in two places:

- **Members** in your organization's sidebar: everyone in the organization,
  invitations and join links. Owners and Admins only.
- **Settings → Team** on a website: who can open that website. Website Admins
  can add people and change roles there.

## Roles

**Organization roles**

| Role | What they can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Everything an Admin can do, plus delete the organization and transfer ownership. Every organization has exactly one Owner. |
| **Admin** | Manage the organization: people, invitations, join links, billing and settings. Admins open and manage **every website**, including future ones. |
| **Member** | Open only the websites they were given, with the role below on each. A Member can also have no website yet. |

**Website roles** (for Members)

| Role | What they can do on that website |
| --- | --- |
| **Admin** | Everything on the website: settings, domains, Git, API keys, readers, add-ons, integrations, and its team. Can't delete the website. |
| **Editor** | Write, comment, preview and publish; manage languages; see the team and usage; one-time exports; their own AI assistant keys. |
| **Viewer** | Read content, drafts, history and analytics, and see settings read-only. |

Give people the smallest role that lets them do their job. Most writers only
need **Editor** on the websites they write for.

## Invite people to the organization

You need to be the Owner or an Admin.

1. Open **Members** and select **Invite people**.
2. Enter one or more email addresses, separated by commas or new lines.
3. Choose the **organization role**: **Member** or **Admin**.
4. For a Member, tick the websites they can open and choose a role for each
   (Editor by default). Choosing none is fine: they join the organization and
   you give them websites later.
5. Select **Send invitations**.

Each person gets an email with a link that works for 7 days, unless invitation
emails are turned off in the organization's **Settings**. The last step shows
whether an email was sent and lets you copy each link. Someone who already
belongs to the organization gets the access straight away, without an
invitation.

## Add someone to one website

Website Admins can do this from the website, without managing the whole
organization.

1. Open **Settings → Team** on the website and select **Add people**.
2. Type an email address. People you already work with are suggested.
3. Choose their role on this website and select **Add people**.

Someone already in the organization gets access at once. Anyone else gets an
invitation to the organization with access to this website only.

## Invitations

Pending invitations are listed with their count under **Members** (and, for one
website, under **Settings → Team**). Each row shows who invited them, when, and
when the invitation expires. Its **⋯** menu offers:

- **Copy invite link**, to send it yourself. Only the invited address can
  accept it.
- **Resend**, which sends the email again and gives the link 7 more days.
- **Revoke invitation**. On a website's Team page this is **Remove from this
  invitation**: it removes only that website from it.

An invitation that was already accepted can't be revoked: the person is a
member now, so remove them instead.

## Join links

A join link lets anyone who has it join with the access you choose, without an
email invitation. Links never make anyone an Admin of the organization.

1. Under **Members → Join links**, select **Create link**.
2. Choose the websites and a role for each. **Viewer** is the default; Editor
   or Admin through a link shows a warning, because anyone the link is
   forwarded to gets it too.
3. Optionally limit it to addresses at one email domain, set a maximum number
   of joins, and choose when it expires.
4. Copy the link: for security it is shown only once.

**Regenerate link** replaces the URL and keeps the settings. **Disable link**
stops it for good. Links that no longer work (disabled, expired or used up) can
be deleted.

Website Admins can also create join links for their website from
**Settings → Team**.

## Accept an invitation or join

The link opens a page that shows the organization, who invited you and what you
get. Sign in, or create an account with the invited address, then accept.

- If you're signed in with another address, the page asks you to switch
  accounts.
- If you already have the access a join link gives, it says **You're already a
  member** and offers to open the website.
- An invalid, revoked, expired or used link says so plainly and offers to sign
  in, create an account, or go to your organizations.

## Change access

- **Organization role:** in **Members**, open a person's **⋯** menu and choose
  **Manage access**. Making someone an Admin gives them every website. Making
  an Admin a Member asks which websites they can still open; their earlier
  website roles are filled in.
- **Websites and roles:** in the same panel, tick or untick websites and change
  the role on each, then select **Save changes**. On a website's **Settings →
  Team**, change the role in the person's role menu.

Owners and Admins appear on every website's Team page as **via organization**;
change their role on the organization's Members page.

When a control is unavailable, Nibleaf says why, for example "Only the Owner
can transfer ownership" or "You can't change your own role".

## Transfer ownership

Only the Owner can do this, and the new Owner must already be an Admin.

1. In **Members**, open the person's **⋯** menu and select **Transfer
   ownership**.
2. Type the organization name to confirm.

You become an Admin, and they become the Owner.

## Remove access

- **From one website:** on the website's **Settings → Team**, select **Remove
  from website** in the person's **⋯** menu. They stay in the organization.
- **From the organization:** in **Members**, select **Remove from
  organization**. They lose access to every website at once, and the join links
  they made stop working.

Their personal AI assistant keys and connected apps stop working on their next
request; see [Work on your docs with an AI assistant](/product/ai-assistants).
