The AI assistant lets readers ask questions about your published documentation
in plain language and get short answers with links to the pages they came from.
It answers only from your published docs. When the docs do not cover a question,
it says so and suggests the closest articles instead of guessing.

## Turn it on

1. Open **Site settings → Smart AI** and connect a provider, or use the one your
   Nibleaf instance provides.
2. Open the site's search settings and turn on **AI answers**.
3. Publish the site, or rebuild its search index, so the assistant can read the
   current version of your docs.

The assistant appears on the published site once the index is ready. To turn it
off, switch **AI answers** off again. Ordinary search keeps working either way.

## What readers see

Readers can open the assistant in several ways:

- the **Ask AI** button in the corner of every page;
- the search box, by choosing **Ask AI** for what they typed;
- **Ask AI about this page** on an article, which focuses answers on that article;
- a link that includes `?ask=` followed by a question, which opens the assistant
  and asks it straight away.

On wide screens the assistant opens as a side panel. On phones it fills the
screen. It follows the reader's language, including right-to-left layout for
Arabic, and the site's light or dark appearance.

Answers start with the direct answer, then give numbered steps with the exact
button and setting names from your docs. The assistant replies in the reader's
language, even when they mix two languages in one question. If a question is too
vague, it asks one short question back, with the options your docs cover.

Each answer shows numbered markers such as **1** and **2** that point to source
cards. Each card opens the exact section of the page the information came from.
Under an answer, the assistant suggests up to three related questions that your
docs can answer. Readers can also ask their own follow-up questions, stop an
answer while it is being written, copy an answer, start a new chat, and rate
each answer.

If the assistant cannot confirm an answer from your docs, it says "I couldn't
verify this from the docs" and lists the articles most likely to help, plus a
**Contact support** link if you set one. While an
answer is being written, readers may briefly see wording that disappears when
the assistant checks its sources. Only checked text stays on screen.

## Suggest first questions

In the site's search settings you can add up to four **Suggested questions**. New
readers see them before they type anything, in the site's default language. Good
suggestions are real questions your readers ask, such as "How do I reset my
password?". Readers in other languages see general starter questions instead.

## Point readers to support

When the docs don't have an answer, readers can reach a person. In the site's
search settings, set **Support link** to your help page (`https://…`) or a support
email address (`mailto:…`). The assistant shows **Contact support** under answers
it couldn't confirm. Leave the field empty to hide the link.

## Review conversations and fill gaps

Open **AI assistant** in the project sidebar. It has three views:

- **Overview** shows how many questions readers asked, how many the assistant
  answered, how satisfied readers were, and the estimated AI cost over time.
- **Conversations** lists each chat. Filter by date, language, rating, or
  unanswered questions, then open a chat to read it, see which pages were cited,
  and read any reader comments.
- **Content gaps** lists questions the assistant could not answer and answers
  readers rated down. Choose **Write an article about this** to start a new page
  for that question.

Project administrators can delete a conversation. Deleted conversations cannot be
restored.

## Privacy and saved questions

**Save questions to improve your docs** controls whether Nibleaf keeps the text
of questions, answers, and reader comments. It is on by default. When it is on,
readers see a short note in the assistant asking them not to share personal
details.

Even when it is on:

- Nibleaf removes email addresses, phone and ID numbers, passwords, keys, and
  similar details before saving text.
- If your site shows a consent banner, text is saved only for readers who accept.
- Saved conversations are deleted automatically after the retention period set
  by your Nibleaf administrator (90 days by default).
- Nibleaf never saves who the reader is.

When the setting is off, Nibleaf still records counts, ratings, and costs so the
overview stays useful, but no question or answer text.

## Limits

The assistant stops answering for the day when the site reaches its daily
question or AI-spend limit, and asks readers to start a new chat when a
conversation gets very long. Readers who ask many questions in a short time are
asked to wait a moment. Your Nibleaf administrator sets these limits; see
[Operate hybrid search and grounded answers](/self-hosting/search-ai).
