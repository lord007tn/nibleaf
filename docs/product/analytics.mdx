Open **Analytics** in your project. Choose a date range and time zone; enable
comparison to see the equally long preceding period. Human metrics exclude
classified bots. A visit is a session in one browser tab, not a unique person.
Visit counts include only readers whose consent is `granted` or `not_required`.
With `denied` or `unknown` consent, content-free page views and search counts
still count, but session and anonymous identifiers are neither created nor
accepted. Page-view totals can therefore include traffic absent from visit totals.

| Section | What it answers |
| --- | --- |
| Overview | What are people reading, where do they arrive from, and what needs attention? |
| Pages | Which articles attract visits, hold attention, or frequently end a visit? Open a page for paths before and after it. |
| Search | What cannot readers find? Compare zero-result searches, result clicks and refinements. Typeahead keystrokes are collapsed into settled searches. |
| Feedback | Which pages receive helpful or not-helpful votes? Low sample counts need care. |
| AI visibility | Which crawlers fetch documentation, and which assistants send human visitors? |
| Performance | Are real readers seeing slow or unstable pages? Headlines use the 75th percentile, not an average. |

An exit is not automatically a failure: a reader may have found the answer.
Engaged time and scroll depth describe observed browser activity, not comprehension.
Performance samples depend on browser support; missing samples are unknown.

## Read AI traffic correctly

`ai_training` collects content for model training; `ai_search` indexes content for
AI answers; `ai_assistant` fetches content live for a user request. `search_engine`
is traditional search, while `seo`, `social`, `monitor` and `other` classify other
bots. Crawler categories describe their claimed User-Agent; **claimed** does not
mean verified identity. Requests are not proof of citations, rankings or training.
Assistant referrals count people following links from assistants, separately
from automated fetches. Markdown, llms.txt, sitemaps and pages are different
resources a crawler may request. Requests served entirely by a CDN or edge cache
never reach Nibleaf origin-side capture and are absent from these crawler counts.

## Why search terms can be hidden

Text is off by default. Only public sites with the project opt-in and eligible
reader consent can retain redacted terms. A term needs at least **2 distinct
visits** before its text appears; others remain in hidden-term totals. Private
sites never show retained raw terms. Sensitive text expires sooner than most
analytics, so counts can remain after text disappears.

**Analytics unavailable** means metrics cannot currently be loaded; your docs
site continues working. Missing values are unknown, not zero. A partial result
has incomplete metrics. If analytics is not configured, ask your operator to
check the [analytics mode](/self-hosting/clickhouse-analytics).
