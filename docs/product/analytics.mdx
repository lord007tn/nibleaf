Analytics answers four questions: what people read, what they can't find, which
articles need work, and how AI tools use your docs. Open your site and select
**Analytics** in the sidebar. For a quick view across all your sites, select
**Analytics** in the main dashboard sidebar.

## Choose a time period

Pick **Last 24 hours**, **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days**, **Last 90 days**, or a
**Custom range** of up to one year. Turn on **Compare with previous period** to
compare with the same length of time just before it. Times are shown in your
browser's time zone.

## What each section shows

| Section | What it answers |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | What are people reading, where do they come from, and what needs attention? |
| **Pages** | Which articles get visits, hold attention, or often end a visit? Open a page to see where readers came from and went next. |
| **Search** | What do readers search for, and which searches found nothing or led nowhere? |
| **Feedback** | Which pages readers marked helpful or not helpful. |
| **AI visibility** | Which AI tools read your docs, and how many people arrive from AI assistants. |
| **Performance** | Whether real readers see slow or jumpy pages. |

To see what readers ask **Ask AI**, open **AI assistant** in the site sidebar
instead; see [Answer reader questions with Ask AI](/product/ai-assistant).

## Read the numbers carefully

- **Visits** count a reading session in one browser tab, not a unique person.
- **Bots are left out** of reader numbers.
- **Consent matters.** If your site asks for consent and a reader declines or
  doesn't answer, their page views still count, but they don't count as a visit.
  So page views can be higher than visits.
- **An exit is not a failure.** A reader may leave because they found the answer.
- **Time on page and scroll depth** show activity, not understanding.
- **A dash (—) means unknown, not zero.**
- **Feedback with few votes** can change a lot from one vote. Wait for more votes
  before you rewrite a page.

## See what readers search for

By default, Nibleaf counts searches but doesn't store what readers type. To see
the actual words:

1. Open **Analytics → Search**.
2. Select **Turn on search terms**, then **Turn on**. Only Admins and Owners can
   do this.

New searches appear as readers make them. Even then:

- a search term appears only after at least 2 different visits searched for it;
  rarer terms are counted as hidden terms;
- private sites never store search terms;
- terms are stored only for readers whose consent allows it;
- Nibleaf removes email addresses, links, and long numbers before storing a term,
  and deletes stored terms sooner than other analytics.

Look for searches that found nothing, and searches where readers searched again.
These often point to a missing article or a word your docs don't use.

## Understand AI visibility

AI tools visit your docs in different ways:

- **AI training** tools collect pages to train AI models.
- **AI search** tools index pages so AI can answer questions later.
- **AI assistants** fetch a page live because a person asked about it.
- **Search engines** are traditional search, such as Google.

Nibleaf identifies these tools by the name they send, which they could fake, so
treat the labels as claims. A visit is not proof that your docs were quoted or
used for training. Separately, **AI assistant** referrals count real people who
followed a link from an assistant such as ChatGPT. Visits answered entirely by a
caching service in front of your site never reach Nibleaf and are not counted.

## When numbers are missing

- **Analytics is taking a break:** figures can't be loaded right now. Your site
  works normally, and the numbers come back on their own. Select **Try again**.
- **Some figures are incomplete:** some numbers couldn't be calculated and show
  as —.
- **No new visits in the last 24 hours:** check that your site is published and
  reachable. New visits usually appear within a minute.
- **Detailed analytics isn't set up on this server:** these insights need
  ClickHouse, a separate analytics database. On a self-hosted instance, ask your
  operator to follow [Operate ClickHouse analytics](/self-hosting/clickhouse-analytics).
