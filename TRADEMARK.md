# Nibleaf Trademark Policy

The Nibleaf source code is free software under the
[AGPL-3.0](LICENSE). The **"Nibleaf" name and the Nibleaf logo are not** —
copyright licenses and trademark rights are separate things, and the AGPL
grants you no rights to the marks.

The point of this policy is simple: when someone sees the Nibleaf name or
logo, they should be able to trust that they are dealing with the Nibleaf
project — not a modified fork or an unaffiliated hosted service.

## You may (no permission needed)

- **Truthfully describe your use** — _"powered by Nibleaf"_, _"built on
  Nibleaf"_, _"compatible with Nibleaf"_, _"a fork of Nibleaf"_ (nominative
  fair use).
- **Self-host Nibleaf**, unmodified or modified, for yourself, your company,
  or your customers, keeping the name and logo as shipped.
- **Link to and write about** the project, including in articles, talks,
  tutorials, and comparisons.
- **Package the unmodified software** for distribution (e.g. an OS package or
  a one-click app template) under the name "Nibleaf".

## You may not (without written permission)

- Use "Nibleaf" or the logo as the **name or brand of a fork**, a derived
  product, or a modified distribution — call it something else and say it is
  "a fork of Nibleaf".
- Offer a **hosted/commercial service under the "Nibleaf" name** (or a
  confusingly similar name, e.g. "NibleafCloud", "Nibleaf Pro") in a way that
  implies it is official or operated by the Nibleaf project.
- Use the name or logo in **domain names, company names, product names, or
  logos** in a way that suggests official affiliation, endorsement, or
  sponsorship that does not exist.
- Modify the logo, or combine the name/logo with your own marks.

## Questions and permission requests

Not sure your use is OK, or want permission for something in the second list?
Email **support@nibleaf.com** — we are generally happy to say yes to good-faith
community use; we only care about preventing user confusion.
