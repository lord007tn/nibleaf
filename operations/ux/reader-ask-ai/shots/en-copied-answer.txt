To serve your docs from your own address, add the domain in Nibleaf, then add two DNS records at your domain provider. [S1]

1. Open **Settings → Custom domain**, enter your domain (for example `docs.example.com`) and select **Add domain**. [S1]
2. Nibleaf shows a **CNAME** record, which points your address to your site, and a **TXT** record, which proves you own the domain. [S1]
3. Add both records in your provider's DNS settings. Use **Copy DNS record** to avoid typing mistakes, then select **Check again**. [S1]

DNS changes usually take a few minutes but can take up to 24 hours. When it's ready, the domain shows **Connected — secure (HTTPS) ✓**. [S2]

You need to be the site's Owner or an Admin, and a subdomain such as `docs.example.com` works best. [S3]
