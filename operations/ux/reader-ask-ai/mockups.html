How do I add a custom domain?

Add the domain in Nibleaf, then add two DNS records at your domain provider.1

  1. Open Settings → Custom domain, enter docs.example.com and select Add domain.1
  2. Add the CNAME and TXT records Nibleaf shows at your provider, then select Check again.1

DNS can take up to 24 hours. The domain then shows Connected — secure (HTTPS) ✓.2
